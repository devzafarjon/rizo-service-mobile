## Review notes and declarations

* **Permissions and why** (use these sentences for store review):
  * Camera: take photos of the device and scan the QR sticker (technician) / attach photos to a request (customer).
  * Photo library: attach existing photos to a request or a job.
  * Internet: all data comes from the RIZO Service server.
  * Phone calls and maps are opened in the system apps (no special permission).
* **Test accounts for the reviewer** (Apple and Google ask for them): create dedicated review accounts on the production server
  (one technician, one admin, one customer) and put them in the review notes. Do not use the demo accounts.
* **Data collected** (Google "Data safety" / Apple "App Privacy"): name, phone number, address, photos of devices, request
  history. Used for the service itself, not for advertising, not sold, not shared with third parties. Data is encrypted in transit
  (HTTPS). Users can ask the service to delete their account.
* **Privacy policy:** both stores require a public URL (`<PRIVACY_URL>`). A privacy policy page is not part of the website yet.
* **Age rating:** 4+ / Everyone. No user-generated public content, no purchases inside the app.
* **Screenshots to prepare:** customer — sign-in, request list, request detail with the estimate, new request, service centers
  (phone, 6.7"/6.1" sizes). Technician — board on a tablet (landscape 12.9"/11"), job screen, estimate builder; admin — dashboard and
  request list on a phone.
* **Version text ("What's new") for 1.0:** "First release."
